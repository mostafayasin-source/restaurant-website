Our story

Cooked over fire, served without hurry.

Ember & Olive is a Mediterranean dining room imagined for Stockholm. The menu stays short: seafood, lamb, vegetables, and a few plates for the middle of the table.

Most of the cooking happens over coals. Olive oil, lemon, herbs, and bread do the rest. The room is for a long meal, not a quick turn of the chairs.

Hospitality is the second source of heat.
